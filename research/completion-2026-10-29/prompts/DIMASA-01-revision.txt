Change ONLY the two standing guards behind the seated king: remove the royal red-gold crossed textiles, yellow royal turbans and jewellery. Give both ordinary undyed cotton wrapped lower garments and muted dark woven shoulder cloths; one simple cream headcloth, the other short bare black hair. Their faces must be distinct ordinary men, one broad round face with short beard, one thin face with moustache, NOT copies of the king. Preserve seated Khorapha, coin, metalworker, scribe, architecture, light and everything else.
