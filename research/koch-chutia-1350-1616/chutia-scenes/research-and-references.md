# Chutia scenes: research, reference selection and production limits

Thirteen scenes generated through built-in imagegen using the current published character sheets as canonical identities. Revised clothing plan read: short sula, churia lower wrap, tongali waist sash and ivory paguri; no male royal neck gamusa/shoulder shawl, no long Mughal coats or fantasy crowns. All these wardrobes and likenesses are project interpretations. Sadhani current published sheet contains a ginger cat; her scene preserves it as a creative continuity choice, not historic evidence. Older lossless masters can be superseded, so the CURRENT published WebP sheet is the actual image input for each named character.

## Sources and precise roles
- https://www.asiguwahaticircle.gov.in/bhismaknagar.html (working image retrieval host without www): ASI describes brick structures in a fortified enclosure. https://roing.nic.in/tourist-place/bhismaknagar/ describes excavated halls/gates and brick/stone/earth rampart. Both official sources differ in chronology from simplified royal attribution. Two real official photographs saved and inspected; district brickwork photo directly supplied to04. They establish material/foundation form, not the appearance of lost complete palaces or Lakshminarayana as certain founder. No readable inscribed brick invented.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chutia_Kingdom : tertiary epigraphic orientation quoting Neog1977, Shin2020 and others, not newly inspected originals. Dhenukhana grant1392 names Satyanarayana as issuer and Nandi genealogically; image01 does not assume joint physical issue. Ghilamara1401 and Sadiya-Chepakhowa1428 retained. No verified original plate photograph located; small copper plates are deliberately oblique reconstruction, without fabricated readable text, exact seal or claimed museum facsimile. Copper is shown freshly worked, not excavation-green patina.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamreswari_Temple :1442 wall inscription, later copper-roof accounts and uncertain surviving layout. https://arunachalpradesh.gov.in/images/Gazetteer/lohit.pdf search extraction transmits Bloch1905 cautions on surviving remains/roof tradition. Full gazetteer not inspected. Scene06 shows wall building, keeps shrine roof outside frame and avoids borrowing later Kamakhya/Hajo form or modern relocated Deori shrine. Deori priest styling is understated interpretation; no sacrificial scene.
- https://or.niscpr.res.in/index.php/IJTK/article/download/3161/2524 : Physico-chemical parameters of traditional salt producing springs of Ancient Assam, Northeast India, IJTK23(2),2024. Downloaded full PDF, relevant methods text and p122 photographs inspected. Describes brine rather than rock-salt mining, clay-coated bamboo evaporation and cylindrical salt; images are comparative modern/abandoned-site documentation, not a medieval photograph. Full-page rendering passed to07 only for method/photo reference. Scene07 shows smith/trader exchange, not a claim all Borhat wells, Sadiya smiths and every workshop occupied one courtyard.
- https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/42192.pdf search extraction supports later Borhat salt-brine use; not direct proof of a particular medieval production layout. Existing generated18th-century smith scene was inspected and supplied as composition/tool comparison only, not historical evidence.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sati_Sadhani and original master CSV distinguish later remembered tradition from contemporary chronicle evidence. Scene11 is a dignified remembered portrait setting, not a documented death, forced marriage, battle action or cliff leap.
- Fate of Sadhaknarayan and identity of last killed ruler differ in accounts;13 paints a qualified departure account, no execution or chains.10 omits a living Dhirnarayan after death.08 shows stockade-building only and omits absent Suhungmung. Royal heirloom cats are inanimate metallic interpretations; do not conflate them with Sadhani living ginger companion.

## Scene review priorities
Distinct royal faces and supporting figures; exact sheet clothing and no added male shoulder drapes; fresh copper and restrained indistinct letters; realistic brick bonds and construction scale; method-specific salt cylinders; modest timber stockades rather than later masonry forts; no unsupported weapon/war machine replicas; regalia modest rather than treasure spectacle; Sadhani face and single ginger cat; people tags reflect who is physically depicted.
