## Metadata decisions
HAJO-05 paints Parikshit receiving news after his accession; dead Raghudev and the absent Man Singha are removed from depicted people, with the historical summary retained. HAJO-07 shows the Mughal fleet near Dhaka; Parikshit is absent and must not be tagged as visible. HAJO-08 shows surrender only; Balinarayan flight remains in narrative but he is not tagged as present in the image. HAJO-01 earlier-temple note will be updated using ASI, since the new checked source identifies old foundations. No unverified exact date or inscription is printed in the images.
