## Not verified (kept out of the scenes or marked low)
- The details of Sadhani's death (common in popular retellings) were not in the sources checked.
- That Rama Saraswati translated under Nara Narayan's commission (usual in literary histories, not in the pages checked).
- Purushottam Vidyavagish and his grammar at Nara Narayan's court: no source found in this session; no scene.
- Hostages after the Treaty of Majuli: not in the sources checked.
- Exact years of most of Chilarai's campaigns.
