The editions discuss damaged texts, alternative readings, and disputed chronology. Their genealogical and royal-activity evidence does not establish these kings' faces, skin tones, ages, individual costumes, or modern community affiliations. All physical designs and clothing assignments below are artistic choices. Consult scans before quoting Sanskrit/OCR wording. Do not infer temperament or achievements from a king's name.

## Catalogue issues to resolve before definitive historical captions

- Current order places Harshadeva after several entries which require verification; the checked epigraphic reconstruction places Harshavarman before Balavarman II and Salambha.
- Arathi is missing from the current character roster despite his identification as Salambha's brother and Harjaravarman's father in S3. Add only after a dedicated catalogue review.
- Do not merge Vijaya and Vigrahastambha without explaining conflicting reconstructions.
- Jivaraja and Digleswaravarman need independent verification. Lack of confirmation in these passages is not proof that they never existed.
- Current reign dates are working catalogue dates, not newly verified facts. Scholarly reconstructions differ, especially for the dynasty's end.

## Costume rules for subsequent sheets

1. No dhoti: use a straight overlapping wrap lungi with a continuous hem at or just below the knee. No crotch tuck, fabric drawn between the legs, ballooned divided legs or trouser-like draping. The Tiwa image's white lower garment is explicitly excluded from copying.
2. Use each supplied image's upper garment shapes, woven borders, scarf treatment, headwrap inspiration and vibrant colours selectively. Do not combine every community motif in one outfit or label a king Tiwa, Rabha or Motok without evidence.
3. Avoid modern plastic buttons, conspicuous button plackets, zippers, tailored pockets, loafers and synthetic shine. Translate closures into cloth ties or concealed overlap. Remove flute, basket and other handheld items for character sheets.
4. Keep subtle royal ornaments and excellent handwoven textile quality. Distinguish kings through faces, proportions, headgear, colour arrangement and shirt silhouette. These differences are creative choices, not historical portrait evidence.
5. Photographic realism: natural pores, age detail, anatomy, woven fibres and soft studio light; no cartoon/CGI finish.
6. Match existing sheet format: top row FRONT, LEFT PROFILE (nose left), BACK, THREE-QUARTER; bottom row FACE CLOSE-UP, RIGHT PROFILE (nose right), LOW ANGLE, HIGH ANGLE. Verify actual angles against captions.
7. Preserve preview-before-catalogue workflow established in this chat. Do not mark sheets completed until approved artwork is saved.
