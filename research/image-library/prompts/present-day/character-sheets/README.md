# Present-day costume character sheets

First batch requested 27 October 2026: the user's 18 distinct attached photographs, made with built-in imagegen. The nineteenth attachment repeats the Mishing woman photograph. Generated sheets live in `images/scenes/present-day/<community>/`; they are local interpretation assets, not published historical person records.

Each sheet has front, side and back full-body views plus face and costume detail views. Clothing and faces follow the supplied photographs. Back construction is inferred and needs community review before being treated as authoritative costume documentation.

`batch-manifest.json` records source photographs, filenames, titles and the prompt template. Two Sonowal women's variants are deliberately retained. The reference labels Matak and Motok are preserved separately as supplied; Singri is an unverified supplied label and must not silently be changed to Singpho.

## Pair coverage in this reference batch

Both man and woman references: Bodo, Karbi, Moran, Rabha, Tiwa. Matak woman and Motok man are supplied under different spelling labels. Woman references only: Deori, Dimasa, Mishing, Singri, Sonowal (two outfits). Missing counterparts require further costume references rather than extrapolating men's clothing from women's clothing.

## Larger roster

The user's eventual scope is Assam's officially listed Scheduled Tribes. These supplied costume labels are not a legal-status roster. The Ministry of Tribal Affairs list is geographically qualified and includes grouped entries, so distinct communities within umbrella entries need explicit tracking. Source: https://tribal.gov.in/downloads/Statistics/NotifiedSTslistsason040823.pdf . Matak/Motok, Moran and Singri must not be marked ST solely because their photographs were included in this batch.

The per-batch manifests `remaining-references-batch-1.json` through `remaining-references-batch-7.json` map outputs to the reference folders and generated source files. The downloaded-reference catalogue and its source credits remain in `research/image-library/present-day-attire/manifest.csv` and `manifest.json`.

These images are visual interpretations from uneven sources, not evidence of an official or uniform costume. The men’s sheets for communities with women-only or textile-only references are provisional. Some references are from neighboring states, festivals, secondary sources, or broad umbrella labels. Hojai Kachari, Synteng, and Khelma have no sheets: there was no specifically labelled Hojai attire reference, no separately verified Synteng outfit, and the Khelma/Biate source caption conflict remains unresolved. Naga and Kuki umbrella communities are represented only by the named subgroup/limited examples stated above.
