# Chandivara / Candivara / Chandibar

Research started 2026-10-27. Existing CSV id: `candivara`.

## Evidence and uncertainty
National Book Trust, December 2018 newsletter, printed p. 6, excerpt from the Sankaradeva chapter of *Warp and Weft*, identifies Chandivara as Sankardev’s great-great-grandfather, a migrant from Gauda under Durlabhanarayana, and a leading Bhuyan landholder. It gives the reign as 1330–50.
Source: https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/attachment/friday-march-8-201911-28-26-amdecember-2018.pdf

The IGNCA-hosted literary history discusses conflicting dating of Durlabhanarayana and links Candivara/Devidasa to land grants. Search-accessible text only; the full PDF could not be fetched by the web reader.
Source: https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/1995.pdf

The existing CSV describes a possible Kannauj origin and 1353 flight. Do not treat those claims as settled or merge them into the Gauda account. Detailed migration routes and exact dates need further comparison. No authenticated likeness or personal costume has been established in this research. Bhuyan leadership should not automatically be portrayed as imperial kingship.
