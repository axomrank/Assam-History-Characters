## Research and limits

Sandhya ruled western Kamarupa and is associated with moving its capital from Kamarupanagara to Kamatapur and the emergence of Kamata in the thirteenth century. Dates differ: Acharyya discusses Yuzbeg's defeat as 1255 and a subsequent move; other reconstructions use 1257 and reign endings of 1260 or 1265. Do not present these dates as settled. Acharyya explicitly calls successor Sindhu Rai his son (p. 152).
