## Main conclusion
There was never one fixed boundary of ancient Assam. Keep four layers apart: epic Pragjyotisa (no usable boundary), historical Kamarupa (inscriptions, Xuanzang), the sacred Kamarupa of the Kalika Purana and Yogini Tantra, and medieval Kamata/Koch/Ahom frontiers (treaties, Buranjis). Evidence order: inscriptions and Xuanzang for political geography; critically edited Kalika Purana for the early-medieval sacred idea; Yogini Tantra for the later four-sided formula; Barua and Gait for the history of interpretation; Lahiri, Ghosh, Shin, Sarkar for modern reconstruction.

## Texts and identifications (as in the data)
- Kalika Purana 53.77b-79a: Karatoya to Dikkaravasini, 30 yojanas broad, 100 long, triangular, dark, many mountains, a hundred rivers. Date about 10th-11th century. Rows: `kamarupa-kalika`.
- Yogini Tantra, Patala XI vv. 16-18: Kanjagiri/Kancana north, Karatoya west, Diksu east, Brahmaputra-Laksha confluence south. Late medieval (16th-17th century). Rows: `kamarupa-yogini`.
- User identifications (2026-11-01): Dikkaravasini = Dikrong; Diksu = Dikhow; Laksha = Shitalakshya; Asurar Ali = Rajgarh Road, south bank, Guwahati. Alternatives in the reports: Diksu = Dikrang or Dibang; Dikkaravasini near Sadiya (27.8 N, 95.6-95.8 E).
- Karatoya: a river corridor through north Bengal and northern Bangladesh; surviving channel point near Bogra 24.855 N, 89.374 E. Kanchenjunga (27.70 N, 88.15 E) is a far anchor only. The old Brahmaputra-Laksha confluence (Mymensingh-Dhaka) cannot be plotted on today's rivers.
- Pithas: Kama, Ratna, Suvarna, Saumara; Sankosh divides Ratnapitha from the next; Rupahi and Bharali appear as other dividers. Internal sacred divisions, not frontiers.

## Ahom frontier sequence (Buranji and Persian record)
1228 Sukaphaa; 16th century Chutia and Kachari lands absorbed; 1639 Barnadi (north bank) and Asurar Ali (south bank); January 1663 Ghilajharighat: Darrang west of the Bharali (north) and the Dimarua-Beltola-Rani zone west of the Kalang (south); 1667 Guwahati recovered; 1679 lost; 1682 Itakhuli, the Manas the western frontier to 1826.

## Other kingdoms
- Koch (Visva Simha): Karatoya west, Barnadi east; reach beyond that under Nara Narayan not annexed territory.
- Chutia: Sisi/Subansiri west (far west uncertain, Biswanath claim contested), Brahmakund/Lohit east, hills north, Burhi Dihing south.
- Dimasa-Kachari (Gait, 13th c.): south bank from the Dikhow towards the Kalang or beyond, with the Dhansiri valley and North Cachar; capital moved from Dimapur to Maibong.
- Jaintia: Khasi-Jaintia hill core, plains toward Jaintiapur and the Surma valley, annexed 1835.
- Kamata: core western and lower Brahmaputra, Karatoya, Cooch Behar; the Mriganka "Karatoya to Sadiya" claim is doubtful.
