## Priority editions still to consult

M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (Gauhati University, 1978): complete Dubi, Nidhanpur, Umachal, Barganga and Nalanda entries. Obtain legitimate access and check exact pages. Also consult the Aphsad inscription edition for Mahasenagupta’s Susthitavarman campaign. These gaps are preserved explicitly in the ruler cards.

## Claims not promoted from search results

Exact accession/death dates, the full “Gauda captivity and release” narrative, death of Susthitavarman in battle, Supratishthitavarman’s alleged reforms or death during his father’s lifetime, specific crown designs, exact textile types, and a Buddhist affiliation for a named early Varman king are not certified by this dossier.

Older online summaries contain contradictions and sometimes omit Ganapativarman or Narayanavarman. Our twelve-person sequence is checked against the primary Nidhanpur translation rather than copied from an abbreviated list.
