## Next sources to consult
- M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam, Gauhati University, 1978: Dubi, Nidhanpur and Nalanda entries. Located as a bibliographic lead, not downloaded or read.
- Xuanzang, Great Tang Records on the Western Regions, Kamarupa account: obtain a scholarly translation and check against Gait’s quotation.
- Bana, Harshacharita: check the embassy and royal-gift passages in a scholarly edition. Gifts alone do not establish the king’s personal wardrobe.
- Museum-catalogued eastern Indian objects dated fourth–seventh centuries for carefully labelled costume comparisons. None yet verified for this pack.

No historical portrait or complete costume reconstruction has been authenticated. No AI image is included as evidence.
