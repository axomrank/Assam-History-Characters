# Varman dynasty — final approved artwork

Approved by the user on 17 October 2026. Thirteen character sheets, in dynastic order from Pushyavarman through Bhaskaravarman. Each is a 1536 × 1024 PNG with eight views. Open index.html for the gallery.

Selected versions: Pushyavarman v2; Samudravarman v1; distinct-face Balavarman I v2 and Kalyanavarman v2; Ganapativarman through Supratishthitavarman v1; Bhaskaravarman v2 with straight frontal face and healthier appearance. Earlier versions remain preserved outside this final folder.

Final means user-approved artwork. These are modern artistic reconstructions, not authenticated historical portraits or wardrobes. Faces, physique, ages, exact regalia and colours are interpretive. Review/reconstruction wording embedded in the selected images is preserved to avoid altering approved images. Research notes retain their original evidence and uncertainty status.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Prompts and the research dossier are included. Bishnu Rabha’s supplied photograph informed Bhaskaravarman’s artistic likeness; it does not establish historical resemblance. Reference photographs are not newly licensed by this approval.
