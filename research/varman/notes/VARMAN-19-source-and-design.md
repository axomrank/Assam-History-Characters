Modern artistic reconstruction of an imagined farm walk. Xuanzang’s Kamarupa passage in Beal’s translation names panasa and narikela (commonly identified as jackfruit and coconut), not banana in that passage. Banana plants are included at the user’s request as an artistic addition. The specific walk, attire and exact travel pack on this occasion are not documented.
