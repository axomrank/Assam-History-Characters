# Thirteen Bengal Pala and Bengal/Delhi sheets

13 selected drafts: 2 Bengal Pala + 11 Bengal/Delhi. Existing Ghiyasuddin Azam Shah preserved. Each sheet has eight views. All selected PNG files opened and verified.

Visual review: complete heads and feet in body views; face and dress continuity across views; opposite profiles; reconstruction labels. Seven initial Sultanate faces repeated the format reference too closely and were revised; original variants retained. Facial identities and precise outfits are invented interpretations, not surviving portraits. Reference chronology and regional limits are documented in research-and-design.md.

Shah Ismail Ghazi remains PROVISIONAL IDENTITY because the 1474 death reference conflicts with the catalogue 1498 attribution. No CSV biography or published media was changed.
