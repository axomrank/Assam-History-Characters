Banglapedia's Bakhtiyar entry, checked in the planning turn, names him as guide: https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Bakhtiyar_Khalji . This supplies narrative context, no visual evidence. Exact route and river identification remain uncertain; no named pass, exact river bend, kilometre scale or precise day is asserted in the new scene.

Their meeting, gestures, spacing, horse types, saddles, clothing and landscape arrangement are staged interpretation. No claim that only the visible number of riders comprised the expedition.
