# Arimatta line sheets — draft set 01

27 October 2026. Created three consecutive first drafts following the user's request for progressively more yellow clothing, younger adult faces, and clearly different identities. They remain unpublished local drafts.

- **Gajanka**, about 32–35 in the artistic design: longer oval face, lean athletic build, early warm yellow-ochre thread accents.
- **Sukranka**, about 27–30: rounded face, full cheeks and clean-shaven appearance; turmeric-yellow and ochre become the main cloth accent.
- **Mriganka**, about 23–26: narrow long face, pointed chin, lean frame and clean-shaven appearance; muted turmeric-yellow/ochre is strongest in this sequence.

This age progression is a user-directed visual device, not historical ages or actual father-to-son chronology. Acharyya's account describes Gajanka as Arimatta's son and Sukranka as Gajanka's son, but dates and some identifications vary across chronicles. The line ends with Mriganka in the common account, while the possible identification with Jangal Valahu remains disputed. The garments and face design are artistic reconstruction only.
