## Individual directions

- **Niladhwaj:** mature, broad and muscular, heavy jaw, short greying hair. Ivory dhoti, charcoal-indigo waist wrap and diagonal sash with muted ochre geometric weaving; structured ivory headcloth with narrow band and small clasp; one armlet and restrained bead necklace. Establish court baseline.
- **Chakradhwaj:** adult son and successor in the common account; share only subtle familial cues with Niladhwaj. Slightly younger and taller, longer face, leaner but still muscular. Keep ivory/indigo dress, introduce a muted rust edge and a slightly finer headband; one pendant and armlet.
- **Nilambar:** adult son and successor in the common account; broad muscular frame, fuller cheeks and commanding posture, clearly distinct face from father and grandfather. Richest cloth quality among the Khen rulers but restrained colors: deeper indigo, charcoal, antique ochre, narrow rust border; modest headwrap clasp, small gold earrings and layered bead-metal necklace. No crown or armor in the turnaround.
- **Sachipatra:** minister, not a king; older muscular but less imposing body, narrower thoughtful face and greying temples. Plainer cream wrap and dark sash, less elaborate headcloth, one modest metal ornament, no royal clasp or kingly necklace. Avoid villain-coded facial features; the story around his family and Nilambar is traditional and should not be illustrated as settled fact.
