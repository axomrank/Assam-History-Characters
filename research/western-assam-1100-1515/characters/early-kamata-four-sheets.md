27 October 2026. Drafts created following user request to finish Singhadhwaj, Pratapdhwaj, Dharmanarayan and Indranarayan. The requested direction: retain a regional darker facial look, vary the faces gradually from Rup Narayan, and increase royal finish gradually from Singhadhwaj through Indranarayan. These appearance cues do not establish ancestry.

## Gradual visual progression

- Singhadhwaj: stockier square face, strong brow and moustache; simple draped attire, narrow woven sash, minimal gold.
- Pratapdhwaj: longer face, slight temple greying; finer cloth, formal headwrap, tilak and sacred thread, a few modest ornaments.
- Dharmanarayan: older lean angular face with greying moustache; layered shoulder cloth, more formal woven edging, medium necklace and headwrap clasp.
- Indranarayan: younger rounded cheekbones, clean-shaven face; finest textile finish in this set, richer but muted sash, small extra necklace layer and simple headwrap accent.

All four are draft sheets under drafts/western-assam/<id>/. Exact garments, sacred thread, tilak, jewellery, ages and physiognomy are creative design decisions.
