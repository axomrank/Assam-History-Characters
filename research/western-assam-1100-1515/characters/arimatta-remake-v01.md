# Arimatta — character sheet draft 01

First draft generated 27 October 2026. The master record places Arimatta (also called Sasanka) around 1365–1385, but this is reconstructed chronology. Later chronicles and historians give conflicting genealogies and legends. N. N. Acharyya recounts Arimatta as a usurper after Indranarayan, but also emphasizes competing stories and uncertain identifications. The sheet avoids literalizing legendary claims.

Design follows the requested Arimatta-line direction: darker regional facial direction with an individual broad-cheeked face, practical heavier draped cloth, broad muted woven borders and minimal ornament. No crown, armor or narrative props. Face, age, clothing, metalwork and hairstyle are artistic choices; no authenticated portrait or costume for him is known from sources consulted.
