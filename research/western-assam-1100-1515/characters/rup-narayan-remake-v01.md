# Rup Narayan character sheet — draft 01

27 October 2026. User directed the Early Kamata rulers to retain the regional face structure and darker skin-tone approach established by Sandhya and Sindhu Rai, while blending woven regional dress with Aryan Hindu royal ornaments. This is a visual direction, not a statement about ancestry or proof of historical appearance.

## Research basis and limits

The current CSV dates Rup Narayan c.1285–1300 and notes that no dates or events are supplied in the cited ruler list. It does not establish his parentage, appearance, religion-specific ornaments or clothing. The names and order of early Kamata rulers are reconstructed from later lists and historical writing. The sheet therefore treats him as a plausible courtly figure, not a portrait.

## Design decisions

Distinct adult man, approximately 40, deep medium-brown skin, broad cheekbones, softly broad nose, almond-shaped eyes, full lips, compact jaw and sturdy build. These are user-directed artistic casting choices, not a claim of ethnic identity. Costume combines an ivory woven dhoti, muted geometric shoulder sash and waist wrap with a plain white headwrap, modest gold earrings and armlets, a small necklace, sacred thread and simple forehead tilak. Each ornament is interpretive; the sources consulted do not verify this specific ensemble for Rup Narayan.

The references to Sandhya and Sindhu Rai were used for the project's chosen visual language and sheet layout. The final image is a first draft and remains unpublished pending user review.
