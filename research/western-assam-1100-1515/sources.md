## Scholarly works to check before publishing (named by the summaries above)
- Barpujari, H. K. (ed.), *The Comprehensive History of Assam*, vol. II (1992): Sarkar's chapters on the medieval period; Kamata; Husain Shah (p. 47); Koch rise (p. 70).
- Gait, E. A., *A History of Assam* (1906); a copy is already in `research/varman/sources/`.
- Neog, M., *Early History of the Vaisnava Faith and Movement in Assam*; Neog 1977 (Chutia inscriptions).
- Nath, D., *History of the Koch Kingdom, c. 1515–1615* and *The Background of Assamese Culture*.
- Choudhury, P. C., *History of Civilisation of the People of Assam to the Twelfth Century A.D.* (for 1100–1200).
- Baruah, S. L., *A Comprehensive History of Assam* (1986) (the Bhuyan destruction of Danyal's garrison).
- Minhaj-i Siraj, *Tabaqat-i Nasiri* (the primary account of 1206 and 1228).
- Sandhyakar Nandi, *Ramacharita* (the conquest of Kamarupa under Ramapala).
