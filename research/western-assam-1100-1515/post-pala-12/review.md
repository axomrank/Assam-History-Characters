## Visual review

All twelve sheets were visually inspected: four standing views with feet visible, frontal and profile head views, low/high-angle head views, and coherent costume/identity within each sheet. Vaidyadeva v02 is selected after removing an unwanted forehead mark; v01 is preserved.

Generated draping differs from some initial straight-wrap proposals: several outputs use pleated/tucked dhoti forms. These are accepted as review concepts for this group, not claims of documented dress; the Salastambha no-dhoti rule does not apply to this separate batch. Small jewellery and textile-border differences remain interpretive. No exact reign dates are printed.

Historical caveats: Kharabana may involve an epithet or name-reading problem; Gokuladeva has competing inscriptional readings; Prithu’s identification is disputed. Samudrapala’s catalogue chronology needs a separate audit. Artwork does not resolve these questions.
