# Storyline gaps filled, 1468–2011

Written 2026-10-28. User rule: a scene is added when the **storyline needs an important event**, not to give every person a
scene. This folder fills the four stretches of the story that had no scenes of their own. `scenes.py` is the single source of the
42 rows (status `planned`), `planned-scenes.csv` is its output, and every row's `sources` cell lists what was used.

Not repeated, because Ahom or Koch scenes already show them: Sankardev's flight (AHOM-243) and his Koch years (KOCH-09, 11, 20);
Gadadhar Singha's persecution of the satras (AHOM-256); the Moamoria rebellion (AHOM-203 on); the sack of Dimapur (AHOM-239, 241);
Rudra Singha's Kachari and Jaintia campaigns (AHOM-188 and neighbours); Bhagyachandra at the Ahom court (AHOM-201, 202);
Yandabo and the end of the Ahom kingdom (AHOM-228 to 233); Joymoti herself (AHOM-174, 177, 281).

## 1. The bhakti movement (BHAKTI-01–06)
Chihna Yatra at Bordowa (traditional, c. 1468) → Madhavdev becomes Sankardev's disciple (1522, or early 1530s) → the prayer house at
Patbausi (after c. 1546) → Madhavdev leads the order and the branches begin (after 1568) → Bhattadev's Assamese prose (c. 1600) →
Aniruddhadeva's Moamara satra (c. 1600), the root of the Moamoria rising.

## 2. Neighbouring kingdoms (DIMASA-01–07, MANIPUR-01–03, JAINTIA-01)
Dimasa: the Dimapur coin (c. 1520) → Maibong capital (1558–59) → tribute to the Koch (1564) → Yasho Narayan beats the Jaintias →
Khaspur capital (c. 1745) → restored by the British (1826) → Cachar annexed (1832–33).
Manipur: the first Maha Ras Lila (1777) → Burmese occupation (1819–25) → Gambhir Singha's return (1825). Jaintia annexed (1835).

## 3. Colonial Assam (COL-01–18)
Tea: the plant shown to Bruce (1823), the Assam Company (1839). Print and language: Orunodoi (1846), Assamese restored (1873),
Jonaki (1889). Revolt and protest: Maniram Dewan and Piyali Baruah hanged (1858), Phulaguri (1861), Patharughat (1894).
The province: Chief Commissioner's province (1874), Eastern Bengal and Assam (1905–12). National movement: Gandhi's tour (1921),
the women's association (1926), Joymoti on film (1935), Kanaklata and Bhogeswari (20 September 1942), Kushal Konwar (1943),
Bordoloi against the grouping (1946), the Sylhet referendum (1947).

## 4. Assam since independence (MOD-01–07)
The 1950 earthquake → the language act (1960) → war at Bomdila (1962) → Fakhruddin Ali Ahmed President (1974) → the Assam Movement
(1979–85) and the Accord (1985) → farewell to Bhupen Hazarika (2011).

## Confidence and gaps
Low: BHAKTI-01, 02, 04, 06; DIMASA-04, 05 (dates not in the sources checked). Single-source details: the Patharughat day.
Casualty figures (Phulaguri, Patharughat) are not stated as fact. Main sources: Wikipedia articles listed in each row, checked
2026-10-28; Assam Tribune and Sentinel pieces for 1858 and 1942 dates.

## Painter notes
Generated images are interpretations. From 1826 on, photographs exist for many people and places: prefer them as references and keep
generated portraits apart from documentary photos. Protest and violence (1858, 1861, 1894, 1942, 1979–85) are to be shown with
dignity, not as spectacle.
